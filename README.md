### Grill 8.5 is here!

[Grill](https://github.com/theadamdanielsson/grill) tests you on your Obsidian notes and remembers what you got wrong. Now your AI agents can use that too.

Claude Code, Codex and Cursor can see your weak spots, explain at your level and test you. Their answers count in Grill. And Chat starts your reviews for you.
